In 1986 a small software company in Europe published a game about the CIA interventions in the Central American nation San Golpe, targeting all major 8-bit home computer platforms of the 80s.

The US government was quick to stop the distribution of the game and keep it locked in government archives, citing national security concerns.

Only now, 40 years later, thanks to the Freedom of Information Act, can the game finally be released to the public.
